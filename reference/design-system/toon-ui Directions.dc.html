One screen, three treatments
Card list, text field, switch and primary button on a 390×844 screen. Press and hold the button to feel press / release; tap the switch for its toggle. Outlines are redrawn on squashed geometry each frame, so the ink keeps its weight and the bottom edge thickens 1.3× on landing.
