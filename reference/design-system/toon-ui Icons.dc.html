01
The rule
1
Keep the metaphor, redraw the line
Start from the Material silhouette, then draw a new centerline inside the 20 dp live area. Never trace the Material path.
2
One centerline per icon
Open or closed subpaths with round caps and joins. No outlined shapes, no filled glyphs. The optional cel fill is a separate closed path underneath.
3
Two weights
Regular 2.1 dp and Bold 2.7 dp nominal. Bold at 16–20 dp, and for the selected item in navigation.
4
Weight follows gravity
The renderer thickens the line low in the grid and on horizontal runs, and thins it high up and on verticals: 0.72–1.44× nominal. Don’t draw the swell by hand.
5
Nothing perfectly round or square
Circles become slightly tilted ellipses (radii differ ~8 %), corners get 1–2 dp of rounding, straight lines may drift 0.2–0.5 dp off axis.
6
Drop what doesn’t read at 24 dp
Inner details under 2 dp, motion lines, extra dots, double contours, Material’s outlined / rounded / sharp variants. Four strokes at most.
7
One overshoot at most
A line end may run 0.5–1 dp past a corner, like the checkbox tick. Never on navigation icons.
8
Colour
The line takes the text colour (ink by day, cream at night). The fill takes one cel colour and marks the selected state.
